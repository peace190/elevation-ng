Our Mission

So that people far from God will be raised to life in Christ.

Who We Are

Elevation NG exists to help you experience God, build community with others, and change the world around you. We believe that no matter your background, you have a place here.


Founded with a vision to see lives transformed, we operate across multiple locations in Nigeria and globally through our online eFam community.

What We Believe

Our beliefs are grounded in the Bible and centered on Jesus Christ.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for all matters of faith and life.

The Trinity

We believe in one God who exists eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Salvation

We believe that salvation is a gift from God, received through faith in Jesus Christ and His finished work on the cross.

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, a community of believers called to love God and serve the world.
